Studio / Workflow

A sales-focused spatial workflow.

Each property is prepared as a coherent visual narrative: first understood in context, then edited, staged, photographed and translated into sales-ready visual assets.

01 / SITE DISCOVERY

Space discovery.

On-site review of circulation, natural light, proportions, existing finishes, architectural strengths and the intended buyer profile. Key visual opportunities and friction points are documented.

02 / PRELIMINARY PRESENTATION

Visual direction.

A concise pre-presentation defines the styling concept, furniture language, material accents, priority interventions, room-by-room staging logic and the level of investment required.

03 / EMPTY-SPACE SHOOT

Base documentation.

The property is photographed before styling to record scale, daylight and architectural character. These images also help plan camera angles, focal points and the final content sequence.

04 / DECORATION + STAGING

Sales-ready styling.

Furniture, lighting, textiles, art and accessories are curated to clarify function, improve perceived scale and build an emotionally legible atmosphere without over-styling the property.

05 / PHOTO + VIDEO

Professional capture.

Final photography and video are art-directed around a shot list for listing platforms, social media and presentations. The sequence balances wide architectural views, lifestyle frames and detail shots.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the studio's real-estate presentation service?

It is a sales-focused service that prepares a property for viewings through spatial editing, styling, decoration and professional visual presentation.

What happens before the decoration phase?

The process begins with a site discovery, followed by a preliminary presentation that defines visual direction, priorities and the staging plan.

Do you provide professional photography and video?

Yes. After styling is complete, the property can be documented with professionally directed photography and video for sales listings, presentations and digital campaigns.

Can this service be used for properties in Milano?

Yes. The workflow is suitable for real-estate presentation and property marketing in Milano and other Italian markets.
